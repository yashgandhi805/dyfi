Types of Mutual Fund Investments

Understanding the basics of each strategy

SIP — Systematic Investment Plan

Invest a fixed amount regularly. Benefit from rupee cost averaging.

SWP — Systematic Withdrawal Plan

Withdraw a fixed amount periodically from your corpus.

STP — Systematic Transfer Plan

Transfer fixed amount from one fund to another periodically.

Lumpsum — One-time Investment

Invest a large amount at once and let it compound over time.
