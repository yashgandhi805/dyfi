What to Look for When Comparing Mutual Funds

1. Returns (CAGR)

Compare 1-yr, 3-yr, 5-yr, 10-yr rolling returns. Don't focus only on recent performance — consistency matters more than one good year.

2. Risk Metrics

Look at Standard Deviation (volatility), Sharpe Ratio (risk-adjusted return), and Beta (market sensitivity). Higher Sharpe = better risk-adjusted performance.

3. Expense Ratio

Lower is better. An expense ratio of 0.5% vs 1.5% can make a significant difference over 10-20 years due to compounding. Prefer Direct Plans over Regular Plans.

4. Fund Manager

Check the fund manager's track record, tenure, and consistency across market cycles. A change in fund manager can impact fund performance significantly.
