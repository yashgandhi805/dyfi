What is SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) allows you to invest a fixed amount regularly in a mutual fund scheme. SIPs harness the power of rupee cost averaging and compounding to build wealth gradually.

How is it Calculated?

SIP returns are calculated using: M = P × [{(1+i)ⁿ - 1} / i] × (1+i), where P = monthly amount, i = monthly rate, n = total months. This assumes a constant return rate.

Important Note

Returns shown are based on an assumed constant annual return. Actual mutual fund returns vary based on market conditions. Past performance is not a guarantee of future results. Please read scheme documents carefully.
