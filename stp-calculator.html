What is STP?

STP lets you move money systematically from one mutual fund to another. Commonly used to deploy a lump sum into equity gradually, reducing the risk of investing at market peaks.

Types of STP

Fixed STP: Fixed amount transferred monthly. Flexible STP: Variable amount based on market conditions. Capital Appreciation STP: Only the gains from source fund are transferred.

Tax Implications

Each STP transaction from the source fund is treated as a redemption and may attract capital gains tax. The transferred amount into the target fund restarts the holding period. Consult a tax advisor.
