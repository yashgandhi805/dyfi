What is SWP?

SWP allows you to withdraw a fixed amount from your mutual fund at regular intervals (monthly, quarterly). It provides a steady income stream while the remaining corpus continues to earn returns.

Who Should Use SWP?

Retirees seeking a regular income, investors with a large corpus needing monthly cash flows, or anyone wanting to manage withdrawals in a tax-efficient manner compared to lump-sum redemption.

Tax on SWP

Each withdrawal is treated as a partial redemption. Gains are subject to capital gains tax (STCG or LTCG) depending on the holding period and fund type. Consult a tax advisor for personalised guidance.
